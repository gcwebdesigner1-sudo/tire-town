What We Do

Full-service auto repair & tires.

We work on all makes and models. Whatever your vehicle needs, we'll give you an honest diagnosis and a fair quote before we touch anything.

Tire Services

New tire installation, rotation, balancing, flat repair, and TPMS service for all vehicle types.

Engine Repair

Full engine diagnostics, motor replacement, intake manifold, head gaskets, and everything in between.

Brake Service

Brake inspection, pad and rotor replacement, caliper service, and brake fluid flush to keep you stopping safely.

Oil Changes

Conventional, synthetic blend, or full synthetic oil changes with a multi-point inspection included.

Transmission

Transmission fluid service, filter replacement, and diagnostic work to keep your drivetrain running smooth.

Diagnostics

Check engine light, warning indicators, and full computer diagnostics to find the root cause — not just the symptom.

Suspension & Steering

Shocks, struts, ball joints, tie rods, and wheel alignments to restore handling and ride comfort.

AC & Heating

AC recharge, refrigerant leak repair, heater core service, and climate control diagnostics for year-round comfort.
